Ask Me Anything

Questions I Answer Every Week

✦

How AI decides who to recommend, and what people ask me most.

01
It Reads

AI systems read your website, reviews, listings, and everything written about you online.

02
It Verifies

Then they check consistency. Mismatched hours, vague services, or thin content lowers confidence.

03
It Recommends

The clearest, most trusted answer gets cited. My job is making that answer you.

Why isn’t my website showing up in ChatGPT?

AI assistants recommend businesses they can read, verify, and trust. If your site lacks clear service descriptions, structured data, and consistent information across the web, you’re invisible to them. The fix is usually structure, not luck.

Does SEO still matter now that AI is everywhere?

More than ever. AI answers are built on top of search results and crawled content. Strong SEO is the price of admission. AI optimization is the layer on top.

Is accessibility really important for small businesses?

Yes, for three reasons. One in four adults has a disability. ADA website lawsuits increasingly target small businesses. And accessible sites tend to rank better. Good ethics, good business.

Should I invest in Google Ads or SEO first?

Depends on your runway. Ads produce results in weeks and stop when you stop paying. SEO takes months and compounds. Most businesses need a mix, and I’ll tell you honestly which comes first for yours.

Why isn’t my website converting visitors?

Usually the page, not the traffic. Slow load times, an unclear next step, or a message that doesn’t match what people searched for. A conversion audit finds the leak before you spend more on ads.

What is schema markup?

Code that labels your content so search engines and AI know exactly what it means: your services, hours, reviews, and location. It’s how machines read your site with confidence, and most small business sites don’t have it.

How do I know if my marketing is actually working?

You should see it in numbers you understand: calls, bookings, and sales, not impressions. If your reports need a translator, that’s a reporting problem, and it’s fixable.

Can AI replace SEO?

AI changes where answers appear, not how they’re earned. Being clear, trusted, and well-structured is still the work. The businesses that do it show up in both.
