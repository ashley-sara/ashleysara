Organic vs. paid in 2026: how to get found now that AEO is on the rise

By Ashley Sara on LinkedIn (opens in new tab) ·October 26, 2026 ·5 min read

The short answer

You need both, but the roles have changed. AI Overviews have cut clicks to top organic results by as much as 58%, while paid ads near AI answers have gained clicks. Use paid search for immediate, high-intent demand, and invest in answer engine optimization (AEO) so AI cites your business. Cited brands earn roughly twice the organic clicks of brands that aren't cited.

For years the organic vs. paid debate was simple. SEO was the slow, compounding investment. Ads were the fast, rented traffic. You picked a mix based on your runway and moved on.

Then Google started answering questions itself. Now there’s a third player in the room: the AI answer that sits above both. And it changes the math.

  • 58%drop in clicks to top-ranking pages when an AI Overview appears (Ahrefs)
  • 2×organic clicks for brands cited in AI Overviews vs. not cited (Seer)
  • +11%paid CTR growth on searches showing AI Overviews (Seer)

What changed in search.

Google’s AI Overviews now appear on a large share of informational searches, and AI Mode has grown past a billion monthly users. Meanwhile, people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for recommendations directly. Searchers get their answer without clicking, which is great for them and painful for websites that relied on those clicks.

But here’s the part most people miss: AI answers still cite sources. Being one of those sources is the new page one.

The numbers: organic vs. paid.

Two big 2026 studies tell the story.

Ahrefs analyzed 300,000 keywords and found that the click-through rate for top-ranking pages fell 58% when an AI Overview was present, comparing late 2023 with late 2025. Even ranking #1 doesn’t guarantee traffic anymore.

Seer Interactive tracked 53 brands and more than 5 million queries from January 2025 through February 2026. Here’s what they saw:

Organic click-through rate on informational searches (Seer Interactive, 2026)
SituationOrganic CTRWhat it means
No AI Overview3.35%The old normal
AI Overview, your brand cited2.07%Fewer clicks, but about 120% more than uncited
AI Overview, brand not cited0.94%Visible but invisible

On the paid side, the trend flipped. When an AI Overview appeared, paid CTR rose from 14.64% to 16.21% over the study period. On searches without one, paid CTR slipped from about 26% to 22%. Ads that sit next to AI answers are catching the people who still want to click.

Bottom line

Organic clicks are shrinking, but citations are the new rankings. Paid is getting more valuable on exactly the searches where AI answers dominate.

What AEO actually is.

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of making your business the source AI tools trust and cite. It builds on SEO; it doesn’t replace it. AI systems pull from crawled pages, reviews, listings, and structured data, so strong SEO is still the price of admission.

What AEO adds:

  • Answer-first content. Lead with a clear, quotable answer, then go deeper. (Like the box at the top of this article.)
  • Structured data. Schema markup that labels your services, location, hours, FAQs, and reviews so machines read you with confidence.
  • Consistency everywhere. Same name, address, hours, and service descriptions on your site, Google Business Profile, and directories. Mismatches lower AI confidence.
  • Proof. Real reviews, case studies, credentials, and named authors. AI rewards evidence.
  • Brand mentions. Being discussed on trusted sites makes you more likely to be recommended.

Where to start this month.

  • Ask the AIs about yourself. Search your top three services in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode. Who gets recommended? Is it you?
  • Check Search Console. Find pages where impressions are steady but clicks dropped. That’s often an AI Overview taking your clicks.
  • Rewrite your top pages answer-first. Put a two- or three-sentence direct answer at the top.
  • Add schema. At minimum: Organization or LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQPage.
  • Move some ad budget to AI-heavy queries. Test paid on informational searches where your organic clicks fell.

Frequently asked questions.

Is SEO dead because of AI Overviews?

No. AI answers are built on crawled, well-structured content, so SEO is the foundation AEO stands on. What's changed is that ranking alone no longer guarantees clicks; being cited in the AI answer matters too.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) helps AI tools like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity understand your business and cite it as the answer. AEO builds on SEO with answer-first content, schema, and consistent information across the web.

Should I spend more on Google Ads now?

Possibly. Seer Interactive found paid click-through rates rose on searches that show AI Overviews. Testing ads on informational queries where your organic clicks have dropped is a smart move.

How do I know if AI is recommending my business?

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode the questions your customers ask. Note which businesses get named and cited. Dedicated AI visibility tools can also track this over time.
