Agentic search is here: how to get chosen when AI agents do the shopping

By Ashley Sara on LinkedIn (opens in new tab) ·October 26, 2026 ·5 min read

The short answer

Agentic search means AI doesn't just answer questions; it takes action, comparing options, booking services, and completing purchases for the customer. Google is building this into AI Mode and Gemini through its Universal Commerce Protocol. To get chosen, your business needs accurate, structured, machine-readable information: prices, availability, hours, policies, and reviews that an agent can trust.

First, search gave us ten blue links. Then it gave us answers. In 2026, it started doing the errands.

“Find me a karaoke room for six on Friday and book it.” “Buy these running shoes when they drop under $100.” “Call around and find a plumber who can come tomorrow.” These aren’t sci-fi prompts anymore. They’re features Google announced this year.

  • 1B+monthly users of Google AI Mode in its first year (Google I/O 2026)
  • 20+retail and payments leaders backing Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol
  • 60B+product listings in the graph Google’s shopping agents draw from

What agentic search is.

A search engine finds information. An agent acts on it. Agentic search combines the two: the AI understands what you want, researches the options, and then completes the task, whether that’s booking, buying, or contacting a business.

That changes who your “visitor” is. Increasingly, the first thing to evaluate your business won’t be a person scrolling your homepage. It’ll be an AI agent reading your data on that person’s behalf, and deciding in seconds whether you make the shortlist.

What Google has already launched.

January 2026: the Universal Commerce Protocol

Google introduced the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), an open standard that lets AI agents handle shopping from discovery to checkout to post-purchase support. It was co-developed with Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target, and Walmart, and endorsed by more than 20 others, including Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Best Buy, and The Home Depot. Alongside it came agentic checkout in AI Mode and the Gemini app, a branded Business Agent that lets shoppers chat with retailers right in Search, and new Merchant Center data attributes built for AI discovery.

May 2026: search agents at Google I/O

At I/O, Google said AI Mode had passed a billion monthly users and introduced search agents that can work in the background: watching for tour dates, tracking prices and buying at a target price, and booking local services or calling businesses for you. Google also announced a Universal Cart that works across Search, Gemini, YouTube, and Gmail, and expanded UCP to hotel booking and local food delivery.

Why this one matters

Hotel booking, local services, and food delivery aren’t just e-commerce. If you run a restaurant, a ranch stay, a salon, or a service business, agents are coming for your category too.

Why it matters for every business.

Agents are ruthless shortlisters. A person might forgive a confusing website or an outdated menu. An agent simply skips you when your information is missing, contradictory, or hard to parse. That means:

  • Your data is your storefront. Prices, availability, hours, service areas, and policies have to be accurate and machine-readable.
  • Trust signals become filters. Reviews, ratings, return policies, and credentials help an agent decide you’re a safe recommendation.
  • Friction kills conversions. If an agent can’t book or buy without a human jumping through hoops, it may choose a competitor that makes it easy.

This is the next layer on top of answer engine optimization. AEO gets you cited. Agent-readiness gets you chosen.

Your agent-readiness checklist.

  • Claim and complete your Google Business Profile. Accurate hours (including holidays), services, service areas, photos, and booking links.
  • Add structured data. LocalBusiness or Organization, Product and Offer (with price and availability), Service, FAQPage, and Review schema.
  • Keep Merchant Center feeds clean. If you sell products, fill out every attribute you can. Google is adding new ones specifically for AI discovery.
  • Publish your policies plainly. Shipping, returns, cancellations, and pricing, in text an agent can read, not buried in a PDF or image.
  • Make booking machine-friendly. Use reputable booking and reservation platforms that integrate with Google, instead of “call us to book.”
  • Match your info everywhere. Same name, address, phone, and hours across your site, listings, and social profiles.
  • Earn fresh reviews. Recent, detailed reviews are one of the clearest trust signals for humans and agents alike.
  • Keep it accessible. Clean, semantic, accessible HTML is easier for assistive tech and for AI agents to understand.

What to watch in 2027.

  • Agents everywhere. Expect agentic booking and buying to spread across more categories and countries as UCP expands.
  • Ads inside agent journeys. Google is already piloting exclusive offers in AI Mode. Paid placement within AI answers is likely to grow.
  • New metrics. “Sessions” and “clicks” will tell less of the story. Track AI citations, agent referrals, and bookings by source.
  • Your own agent. Brands will increasingly run their own AI assistants (like Promise, on my site) that talk to customers and to other agents.

Frequently asked questions.

What is agentic search?

Agentic search is when an AI doesn't just answer a question but takes action on it, such as comparing options, booking a reservation, or completing a purchase on the user's behalf. Google is building this into AI Mode and the Gemini app.

What is Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)?

UCP is an open standard Google announced in January 2026 that lets AI agents handle commerce from product discovery through checkout and post-purchase support. It was co-developed with Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target, and Walmart.

Do small and local businesses need to worry about AI agents?

Yes. Google's agents can book local services and call businesses, and UCP has expanded to hotel booking and food delivery. Accurate Google Business Profile data, structured data, and easy online booking help agents choose you.

How do I make my website ready for AI agents?

Keep prices, availability, hours, and policies accurate and in plain text, add schema markup, keep your listings consistent everywhere, use booking tools that integrate with Google, and build accessible, semantic pages that machines can parse.
